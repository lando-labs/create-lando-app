## The problem (validated in the wild)
The getting-started page hands users off to their AI, but the handoff is rough:
the page masks its saved theme (slate `:root` override) so the theme looks broken
until the page is deleted into a void; cold "build X" prompts skip orientation; and
the "protected layout" framing makes the AI route around `layout.tsx` by reflex.
`sst-salesiq` (a real app on the scaffold) rebuilt the entire preview as a
`/theme-preview` route to work around the masking — its own commit says so.
