### Ordering Rationale

The page is the debut surface, so it must be *good* before v0.1.0 goes public —
publishing the current "gallery" page would be a weak first impression for the
whole funnel. Sprint 4 is deliberately one large-but-coherent unit because the
colour engine and the page that hosts it are inseparable. Launch (Sprint 5)
follows immediately once the page is right. Vite (Sprint 6) is post-launch, same
as the plan has always sequenced second templates.
