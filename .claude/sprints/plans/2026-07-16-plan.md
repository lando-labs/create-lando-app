not a fragment. So Sprints 1–2 are dev work merged to `main` (no release), and
Sprint 3 publishes v0.1.0 once the experience is coherent. (Alternative —
incremental public releases per sprint — was considered and set aside: the front
door *is* the product; debut it whole.)

### Ordering Rationale

Strict dependency order: the migration (#10) is the taproot — every other issue
assumes the scaffold resolves the real DS. #11 completes the CLI foundation on
that fixed base. Then the two halves of the experience — the AI's orientation
(Sprint 2) and the human's (Sprint 3) — build on the foundation. The human page
comes last because #14 renders *from* the brief authored in Sprint 2 (single
source), so the brief must exist first. v0.1.0 publishes at the end of Sprint 3
when the whole front door is real.
