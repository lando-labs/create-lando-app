## The organizing constraint: the A1 gate

Everything a scaffolded app does is blocked on `@lando-labs/design-system`
going **public on npm** (the "A1" launch, owned by the DS repo). So the plan is
sequenced as: **build everything that doesn't need A1 first** (Sprint 1), hold
the A1-gated launch as a tight, pre-verified sprint (Sprint 2), then grow the
surface post-launch (Sprint 3).

### Ordering Rationale

The A1 gate splits the world into blocked/unblocked. Sprint 1 front-loads
**all** the unblocked, high-leverage work (the smoke test is the real drift
guard; a plain version bump can't catch a layer-contract break) so that Sprint 2
— the actual launch — is reduced to flipping dormant scaffolding live, one
validation pass, and a tag push. Sprint 3 is deliberately post-launch: don't
add a second-class Vite template before the flagship Next path is proven in
public.
