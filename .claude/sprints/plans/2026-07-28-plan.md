## Context

**`@lando-labs/create-lando-app@0.1.0` is LIVE on public npm** (scoped, trusted
publishing + provenance), repo is public, and Dependabot now tracks the DS pin.
The debut is out.

A real first-run walkthrough (owner scaffolding a live app, plus the `sst-salesiq`
repo built on the scaffold) surfaced a cluster of **front-door DevX** rough edges —
all confirmed by real usage, not speculation:

1. The getting-started page **masks its own result**: `ColorFoundation` pins `:root`
   to a slate baseline, so a saved theme is invisible until the page is deleted —
   into a void. `sst-salesiq` proves it (commit `164980b` rebuilt the whole preview
   as a `/theme-preview` route "because … the brand theme is invisible there").
2. Cold "build me X" prompts **skip orientation**, yielding worse, less DS-idiomatic
   code; telling the AI to *get familiar first* fixes it.
3. Every DS MCP call hits a **permission prompt**, breaking the "just talk to your AI"
   flow.
4. The "protected root layout" framing pushes app chrome into `app/(app)` route groups
   **by reflex** — a valid structure, but emergent rather than taught.

These are the immediate theme. Backlog groomed: closed #4 (published), #36 (shipped),
#28 (page-v2 epic done), #7 (launch tracking done). Findings folded into #51.

### Ordering Rationale

The scaffold is public and being used *right now*, so the highest-value work is
smoothing the first-run friction real users just hit — the handoff (Sprint 1) is the
biggest and most-validated (a real repo rebuilt the preview to work around it). MCP
pre-approval (Sprint 2) is small, independent, and removes the other constant
first-run annoyance. The Vite template (Sprint 3) is deliberately last: ship a great
Next handoff before cloning it into a second template.
